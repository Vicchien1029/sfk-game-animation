# Animation Curriculum Models V2 — ImageGen Prompt Set

The two final assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool through independent calls. `role-v2-production-2d-animation.png` was supplied only as the visual-style reference; neither final scene copies its content or character. An earlier advertising-oriented commercial-animation draft was rejected after the course definition was corrected and is not referenced by the page.

## Shared production specification

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset type: `website curriculum section illustration`
- Bright, refined and professional cartoon animation-production illustration.
- Clean confident outlines, simplified mature forms, polished 2D/3D hybrid shading and the same editorial studio visual language as the animation-role V2 set.
- Square master composition with essential people, tools and actions inside the central 55%, safe for both landscape and narrow center-cover crops.
- Soft bright light; vivid but controlled orange/coral, sky blue, teal, warm yellow and lavender accents; never neon.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, readable interfaces, real brands or third-party IP.
- Avoid photorealism, dark cinematic lighting, heavy concept-art treatment, clutter and extreme edge placement.

## Commercial Animation — Final V3

Create professional industrial production for theatrical animated feature films and television animation series. Show a coordinated team—director, character animator, environment artist and production artist—reviewing and developing one story sequence together. A large central screen displays an original cinematic animated scene with expressive characters in a fully designed environment; image-only shot panels, character poses and environment frames make the film/series pipeline and collaboration unmistakable. Exclude product display, advertising storyboards, brand campaigns, packaging and marketing symbols.

## Interactive Media Art

Create an immersive media installation where an artist configures an animated projection environment and a visitor’s body gesture activates flowing original cartoon forms, particles and responsive light across a curved projection wall and floor. Include visible spatial sensors and a projection device so real-time sensing, audience participation and spatial animation are unmistakable rather than a generic technology interface.
