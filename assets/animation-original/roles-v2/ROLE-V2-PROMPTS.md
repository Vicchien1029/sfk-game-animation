# Animation Roles V2 — ImageGen Prompt Set

All 15 assets were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool through 15 independent calls. Each call used the shared visual baseline below plus its own role-specific scene.

## Shared visual baseline used in every call

- Use case: `stylized-concept`
- Asset type: `website section illustration`
- Polished, professional cartoon animation-production illustration.
- Clean confident outlines, simplified rounded forms, refined 2D/3D hybrid shading, and one consistent editorial animation-studio visual language.
- Square composition with one principal worker and the role-defining action centered in a medium-wide view; all important content stays inside a generous 12% safe margin and remains readable with a center `cover` crop.
- Soft bright studio lighting; vivid but controlled orange/coral, sky blue, teal, warm yellow and lavender accents with deep neutral grounding; colorful but not neon.
- No text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, brand interfaces, known film/animation IP, or third-party characters. Screens and interfaces are abstract and unlabeled.
- Avoid photorealistic photography, dark cinematic lighting, heavy concept-art mood, clutter, illegible interfaces, and anatomical errors.

## 01 — Scriptwriter / Screenwriter

Show a focused animation writer arranging story beats and character arcs with blank color cards, an open sketch notebook, pencil and a small tablet. Make the storytelling process visually obvious through original character silhouettes, scene thumbnails and connected story-beat cards without written language.

## 02 — Character Designer

Show a character artist using a pen display to design an entirely original friendly fantasy creature, with expressive pose silhouettes, facial-expression studies and a simple turnaround visible as image-only shapes. Include drawing tools, unlabeled color swatches and a small sculpted maquette.

## 03 — Environment Designer

Show an environment artist designing a colorful original animated world: a stylized town and landscape in perspective, thumbnail compositions, a simple 3D blockout and visual scale references. The artist actively paints on a pen display.

## 04 — Storyboard Artist

Show a storyboard artist arranging a clear sequence of five image-only panels depicting an original character moving through a simple action while drawing the next shot. Camera changes and action flow must be understandable from the pictures alone.

## 05 — Animatic Editor

Show an animatic artist timing image-only storyboard shots on a monitor. Place sequential frames above a timeline made of colored blocks and abstract audio waveforms while the artist adjusts pacing with a stylus and wears headphones.

## 06 — 2D Animator

Show a 2D animator drawing key poses of an entirely original lively character on a pen display, with several semi-transparent onion-skin poses that clearly communicate motion arcs and frame-by-frame progression.

## 07 — 3D Animator

Show a 3D animator posing an entirely original stylized creature in an unlabeled 3D viewport using abstract rotation rings and motion-curve handles. Add a second pose preview to make performance and body mechanics obvious.

## 08 — Character Modeler

Show a 3D character artist sculpting an entirely original stylized creature on a large screen, with a clay-like maquette, clean topology lines and a turntable view that makes volume and form construction obvious.

## 09 — Character Rigger

Show a technical animation artist building a skeletal rig inside an entirely original stylized character. Clearly show simplified bones, joints, facial controls and colorful controller rings while the artist tests a pose.

## 10 — Stop-Motion Animator

Show a stop-motion animator carefully adjusting an original handmade puppet on a colorful miniature set beneath a camera and soft studio lights. Include replacement facial pieces, tiny props and an unlabeled frame-capture setup.

## 11 — Visual Effects Artist

Show a VFX artist shaping stylized fire, water, smoke and sparkling particles around an entirely original animated creature using image-only simulation controls and abstract node connections.

## 12 — Lighting Artist

Show a lighting artist shaping a three-point lighting setup for an original animated character and miniature digital set. Make warm key light, cool fill light and a bright rim light visible as clean colored light cones while the artist adjusts virtual lamps.

## 13 — Rendering Artist

Show a rendering artist reviewing several clean visual passes of one original animated scene: beauty, shadow, material and color passes displayed as image-only tiles while adjusting render quality. Communicate rendering and pass review rather than editing or compositing.

## 14 — Compositor

Show a compositor combining an original animated character, painted background, atmosphere, glow and color treatment. Represent the elements as several transparent visual layers floating toward one finished image on a monitor.

## 15 — Editor and Sound Designer

Show an editor wearing headphones assembling image-only animated shots on a video timeline while balancing colorful audio waveforms and a small mixing console. Include speakers, a compact microphone and tactile control knobs.
